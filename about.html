Our Story

Family-Owned Since 2011

Aspen Auto started as a small three-car lot with one promise: sell quality vehicles at a fair price, and treat every customer like a neighbor. Fifteen years later, that promise hasn't changed — even as we've grown into one of the Roaring Fork Valley's most trusted pre-owned dealers.

We hand-select every vehicle on our lot, put it through a 150-point inspection, and back it with a real warranty. No hidden fees, no pressure tactics — just straightforward car buying.

Transparency First

Upfront pricing with no surprise fees or last-minute markups.

Quality Over Quantity

We hand-pick every vehicle — if we wouldn't drive it, we won't sell it.

Community Rooted

Locally owned and proud to serve the neighbors who keep us going.
